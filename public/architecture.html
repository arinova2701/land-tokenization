NyayaChain architecture

A signed court order moves through the browser and the Besu chain. The coordination service carries pending orders, but it is an untrusted off-chain mailbox: the browser and contract independently validate the signatures and the chain decides acceptance.

Freeze sequence

  1. Judge selects a parcel, case number, expiry and current judge nonce. The browser computes keccak256(abi.encode(tokenId, caseNumber, orderExpiry, nonce)).
  2. Judge, Clerk and Registrar sign the 32-byte digest using the EIP-191 personal-message prefix. Signatures can travel through the untrusted mailbox.
  3. The browser previews checks with getOrderChecks and submits the order and signatures to CourtKeyRegistry.
  4. The contract recovers each signer and checks the on-chain registry, quorum, jurisdiction, value, expiry, replay and nonce. It then calls ParcelToken as its agent.
  5. Besu records the transaction, acceptance event and history entry. The citizen page and independent verifier read that on-chain proof.
